METHOD

Two reasoning problems.
Two distinct evaluations.

Recover the assembly hierarchy from the manual, then study spatial refinement under a correct-operation protocol.

01 / STRUCTURE

Anchor the plan in the manual.

A semantic part table grounds numbered observations in names and functions. Boundary anchors and ordered step masks help track new parts and carried-over subassemblies. The textual plan is converted into a nested assembly tree.

EXAMPLE HIERARCHY · ILLUSTRATIVE[[part_0, part_1], part_2]
First subassemblyThen combine

Evaluation input: precomputed numbered step masks. Raw-manual mask generation is outside the reported evaluation.

02 / PHYSICAL STATE

Observe the after-state. Refine the pose.

Multi-view observations ground a moving part relative to the fixed assembly. Rendered after-states expose errors such as floating, collision or wrong-side attachment. Feedback conditions incremental translation and rotation corrections.

T̂r+1 = δTr T̂rSTATE-CONDITIONED SE(3) UPDATES

Evaluation input: correct operation and preceding state. Each prediction is scored before ground truth constructs the next context; this is not an end-to-end rollout from a predicted tree.
