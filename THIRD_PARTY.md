# Attribution and third-party assets

The code derives from **Manual2Skill: Learning to Read Manuals and Acquire Robotic Skills for Furniture Assembly Using Vision-Language Models**. Its inherited Apache License 2.0 is retained verbatim in `LICENSE`; the original author attribution and upstream links are preserved in `docs/upstream/Manual2Skill_README.md`.

Five Bench/applaro illustrative input images are published at the project owner's explicit request. Their original pixels are retained and their source paths/hashes are documented in `website/assets/PROVENANCE.json`. IKEA manual illustrations and IKEA-Manual-derived rendered inputs retain their original third-party rights; the code's Apache-2.0 license does not relicense those images. The full dataset, PDF originals, meshes, and local data archives are not distributed.

Seven figures from the author-supplied AssemState manuscript are displayed on the project page. PDF figures are rendered to PNG; the existing pose-pipeline PNG is copied unchanged. Source and published-file hashes are in `website/assets/paper/PROVENANCE.json`. Paper figures and embedded third-party imagery retain their original rights and are not relicensed under the code's Apache-2.0 license.

Archived model-generated responses and experiment measurements are research artifacts. Paper overview diagrams are conceptual, not recorded successful model trajectories. The retained three-round Bench/applaro trace is a failure example.
